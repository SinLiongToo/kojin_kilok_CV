After days and weeks of intense collaboration, brainstorming, and iterative coding, I finally finish my 3rd tool for my usage! 🎉

I poured a lot of deep thought and hard work into solving some of the most frustrating bottlenecks in test program validation, and I have successfully launched the Semiconductor STDF Sanity Checker! 🚀

This tool was born out of real-world engineering pain points. I wanted to completely eliminate the tedious manual key-ins, layout plotting, and slow data load times that product engineers face daily.

It required a lot of deep thinking—from optimizing memory slice pivots to achieve sub-second performance on millions of rows of STDF data, to refactoring yield metrics, to designing a customizable Cpkn/Delta-Sigma A-F health grouping dashboard.

It has been an incredible journey of collaborative pair-programming, and I’m proud to share this game-changing solution:

🚀 Tired of wasting hours manually verifying STDF data and waiting for slow scripts?
In semiconductor product engineering, validation is everything. But let's be honest: comparing test program versions, product variants, or consolidations like test stage CP1/CP2 can feel like a major bottleneck.
Hours of tedious manual key-ins, layout plotting, and file loading shouldn't stand between you and your NPI release.
The Semiconductor STDF Sanity Checker is a unified, one-click verification platform that transforms data validation from hours of manual labor into sub-second insights.

Here is how it accelerates your engineering workflow:

💡 NPI Development Acceleration: Instantly compare new silicon runs against golden references to speed up time-to-market.
🔄 Seamless Version Migrations: Spot differences in limits, test numbers, units, and yield correlations side-by-side.
🧬 Variant & SKU Comparisons: Quickly validate parametric and binning distributions across different device variants.
🎯 Test Stage Consolidation & Elimination: Confidently consolidate or eliminate test stages (e.g., verifying CP1 to CP2 transitions) by verifying coverage with robust statistical backing.

Why engineers love it:
✅ One-Click Automated Reports: No more custom script writing or tedious manual key-ins.
✅ Sub-Second Caching: Built-in optimized PKL gzip caching loads huge STDF data in under a second.
✅ Interactive Wafer Maps & Pareto Dashboards: Instantly trace ECID changes, yield drops, and bin state transitions with real-time hovering.
